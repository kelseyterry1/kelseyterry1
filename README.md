# Hi, I'm Kelsey 👋

**VP of Product @ CompanyCam · building agentic products with Claude** <!-- placeholder headline — swap freely -->

I'm a product leader who still builds. By day I run product at [CompanyCam](https://companycam.com); by night and weekend I ship real software with agentic AI — because the best way to lead AI-first teams is to build with the tools yourself, not just talk about them.

Fifteen years in, across consumer electronics, mobile, fintech, e-commerce, and government. I've learned that great products come from staying close to the craft. So I stayed close.

## What I'm building

- **AI-first product at [CompanyCam](https://companycam.com)** — construction SaaS that helps contractors capture the great work they do, so they deliver higher-quality outputs and win more business off the strength of it.
- **The AI enablement team** — I own the agentic harness that makes our PMs and designers dramatically more effective. The result: **PMs now ship their own changes to production.** Product people who build, not just spec.
- **[Intentional](https://www.beintentional.app)** *(pre-launch)* — a consumer app I'm building almost entirely through agentic development with Claude and Codex. It helps you be a better friend in an increasingly digital age: identify the people who matter, actually make time for them, remember the thoughtful little details of their lives, and show up prepared and present every time you connect.

## How I work

- **AI-native, hands-on.** I build with agentic development every day — Claude and Codex are core to how I write specs, prototype, and ship, not a side experiment.
- **PM who codes.** I close the gap between product and engineering by living in both. Ideas become working software fast.
- **Enablement over gatekeeping.** I'd rather build the harness that lets a whole team ship than be the bottleneck they wait on.
- **Craft + outcomes.** Warm on the team, ruthless on the result. The point of all of it is durable, high-value products people love.
